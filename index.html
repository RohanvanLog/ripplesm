Privacy Policy — RippleSM

Effective Date: 1 February 2026

RippleSM (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you interact with our WhatsApp chatbot service.

This service is used to qualify leads for solar panel installation businesses in South Africa.

1. Information We Collect

When you communicate with RippleSM via WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Phone number (required to respond)
  • Message content you send to the chatbot
  • Name, address, or other details you voluntarily provide during lead qualification
  • Basic interaction metadata (such as timestamps and conversation flow)

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless you choose to provide it.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Qualify solar installation leads
  • Respond to your questions and requests
  • Provide relevant information to solar panel installer business owners
  • Improve chatbot performance and customer experience
  • Maintain platform safety and prevent abuse

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Retention

To provide conversation context and continuity, we may store WhatsApp chatbot messages and user details in a secure internal record system, including tools such as Google Sheets.

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to:

  • Complete lead qualification and referral
  • Support service improvement
  • Meet legal or compliance requirements

You may request deletion of your data at any time.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share limited personal data only in the following cases:

4.1 Service Providers

We use Google Gemini (LLM service) to generate automated responses. Message content may be processed by Gemini strictly for chatbot functionality.

4.2 Business Referrals

Qualified lead details may be shared with relevant solar panel installation business owners for follow-up contact.

We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

5. User Rights (South Africa)

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Object to unnecessary processing

To exercise these rights, contact: mia@ripplesm.com

6. Security Measures

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect user information, including access controls and secure handling procedures. However, no online platform can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Platforms

This chatbot operates using the WhatsApp Business Platform. Your use of WhatsApp is also governed by Meta’s own privacy policies and terms.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
